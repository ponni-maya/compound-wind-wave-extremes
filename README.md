### About Me

PhD in Civil Engineering (Hydraulic and Offshore Engineering) from **TU Delft**, currently a **Guest Researcher** there continuing first-author publication work after successfully defending my dissertation in June 2026. I hold an MSc in Physical Oceanography (Cochin University of Science and Technology, dissertation at IITM Pune) and have spent the last ~5 years turning large multi-model climate and metocean datasets — ERA5, CMIP5/CMIP6, ScenarioMIP, HighResMIP, WAVEWATCH III — into physically interpretable indicators of climate variability, compound extremes, and infrastructure-relevant risk.

My work connects physical climate drivers to consequences for offshore energy, operational downtime, infrastructure design, and flood risk. I'm now looking to extend this foundation toward **ISIMIP-based attribution, cross-sector climate-impact indicators, and integrated assessment of exposure, risk, and damages**.

### 🔬 Current Project — Compound Wind–Wave Extremes

A regime-based framework comparing ERA5, ScenarioMIP, and HighResMIP compound wind-wave extremes across European seas:

| Stage | Focus | Key methods |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Frequency & intensity of compound extremes | Joint exceedance probability (P95∩P95), threshold sensitivity |
| 02 | Model consistency & bias in extreme tails | GPD fitting, percentile comparison, Taylor diagrams |
| 03 | Dependence structure of joint extremes | Kendall's τ, empirical tail dependence, Gaussian copulas |
| 04 | Temporal persistence & downtime | Run-length statistics, Downtime Risk Index (spatial) |
| 05 | Extreme–mean coupling & intermittency | EMCR (p95/mean) across regimes and variables |
| 07 | Joint design return levels | Copula-based return level contours, model bias in design values |
| 08 | Synthesis across regimes | Cross-metric summary heatmaps, ERA5 vs. model deltas |

Full paper version: *"Inter-Model Evaluation of Compound Wind-Wave Extremes Across ERA5, ScenarioMIP, and HighResMIP for European Seas"* (manuscript under revision, 2026).
